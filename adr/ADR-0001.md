# ADR-0001: Separate Tutor Brain policy from reasoning engines

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

LLM providers differ in availability, capability, price, and behaviour. Embedding tutoring policy in provider-specific system prompts makes the product brittle and unauditable.

## Decision

The Tutor Brain owns schemas, deterministic decisions, workflow transitions, and validation. Providers are accessed through a capability-based adapter; prompt files are provider-neutral.

## Consequences

Adapters add integration work and may expose different output quality. In return, providers can be switched, evaluated, or used selectively without migrating student state or policy.
