# ADR-0002: Use append-only learning evidence with derived summaries

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-07-19

## Context

Mastery estimates, feedback, and revision schedules all change as new evidence arrives. The
algorithms that produce them will also change: interval policy will be recalibrated, and
mastery computation will be revised as the system learns what predicts recall.

## Problem

If evidence is overwritten by its own conclusions, three things become impossible:
recalculating history under an improved algorithm, explaining to a student why the system
believes what it believes, and correcting a bad inference without losing the observations that
produced it.

Storing only current mastery is lossy in exactly the direction that matters.

## Decision

Validated learning events are stored as **append-only records**. Profile mastery, retrieval
indexes, and plan summaries are **versioned derived views** that carry references to their
source events and can be rebuilt from them at any time.

The [Student Model](../docs/07%20Student%20Model.md) is a projection of
[Memory](../docs/08%20Memory%20Architecture.md), never the reverse.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Mutable current-state record only | Smallest storage and simplest reads, but recalibration and dispute resolution become impossible. |
| Full event sourcing with no materialised views | Maximum fidelity, but every read requires a replay; unacceptable for in-session latency. |
| Append-only events with a retention window | Bounds storage, but silently caps how far back a recalibration can reach — the cap would be invisible at review time. |
| Overwrite with an audit log | The audit log becomes a second source of truth with no schema discipline; drift is guaranteed. |

## Tradeoffs

**Accepted cost:** higher storage volume; projection logic must be written and maintained;
derived views can go stale and need explicit rebuild paths; erasure is more complex because it
must reach both source and derived records.

**Gained:** auditability, recalibration under improved algorithms, correction without loss,
and a defensible answer to "why does the system think I am weak at this?"

## Consequences

- Deletion requests MUST erase source and derived records together
  ([Constitution §8](../docs/01%20Tutor%20Constitution.md#8-failure-modes)).
- Contradictory records coexist rather than overwriting; resolution follows Constitution C5.
- `memory.json` carries `derived`, `derived_from`, and `superseded_by`; records are never
  rewritten in place.
- The Student Model's `derived` block is explicitly non-authoritative and MUST NOT be edited
  directly.

## Future Considerations

- Storage growth may eventually justify cold-tiering old ledger records; this is a storage
  concern and MUST NOT become a retention cap on recalculability.
- Rebuild cost for derived views will grow with history length and may require incremental
  projection.
- A future ADR may specify snapshot checkpoints to bound replay cost without discarding events.
