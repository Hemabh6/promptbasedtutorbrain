# Monthly Workflow

**Trigger:** calendar month boundary or student-selected cycle start.

1. Review syllabus coverage, mastery evidence, answer rubric trends, and plan adherence.
2. Identify bottlenecks by evidence quality, not mere time spent.
3. Set measurable monthly outcomes and high-level topic allocation.
4. Reserve mock/answer practice and revision capacity around known deadlines.
5. Create weekly planning inputs; do not create an unreviewable month-long daily schedule.

**Exit:** confirmed objectives, capacity assumptions, risks, and the first weekly planning trigger.
